DEFINITIONS
- competitor is a normalized competitor name. competitor_mentions counts every mention on a call, so it is always greater than or equal to the number of calls mentioning that competitor.
- Save Rate is the share of cancellation-flagged calls whose disposition is "retained".
- Revenue at Risk is recurring revenue on calls that named a competitor AND requested cancellation.

RULES
